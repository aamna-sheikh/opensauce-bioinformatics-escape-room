# Cracking the Code: A Bioinformatics Escape Room

An interactive bioinformatics escape room developed at Northeastern University, Oakland to introduce bioinformatics concepts through collaborative, puzzle-based learning.

## About the Project

This project was co-developed with Dr. Jennifer Cassano as an interactive approach to bioinformatics education and public science engagement. Participants work through a series of puzzles that introduce concepts including DNA decoding, sequence alignment and comparison, computational analysis, and computational thinking.

The escape room was first piloted with students and faculty at Northeastern University, Oakland and refined based on participant feedback. It was publicly implemented at Open Sauce 2026 at the San Mateo Convention Center and will also be presented at the Bay Area Science Festival at UCSF Mission Bay in October 2026.

## Research

The escape room is part of an ongoing bioinformatics education research study. We obtained approval from Northeastern University's Institutional Review Board (IRB #26-05-09) prior to public implementation and collected anonymous pre- and post-activity survey data to evaluate participant engagement, understanding of bioinformatics, and the educational impact of the activity.

Survey analysis is currently underway, and a peer-reviewed manuscript describing the design, implementation, and evaluation of the escape room is in preparation.

## Repository Contents

This repository contains materials used to develop and implement the bioinformatics escape room, including puzzle materials, educational resources, and supporting files.
